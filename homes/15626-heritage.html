About this home

Curb appeal that stops you at the street, & a private gated courtyard that welcomes you in. This well cared for Safford sits on a corner lot in Sun City West with mature professional landscaping plus block & wrought iron fencing creating a secluded feel front & back. Out back, an extended paver patio with a built in barbecue turns the yard into an outdoor room. Inside, you will find upgraded kitchen & bath cabinetry, granite counters throughout, stainless appliances & a reverse osmosis system. The open kitchen offers real workspace & flows to the living & dining areas. Two bedrooms, two baths, a separate den or office & an expanded laundry room. Tile roof & two car garage. Residents enjoy golf, pools, pickleball, fitness & over one hundred clubs. A special find, inside & out.
